Pipeline & Method

My Animation Process

01

Thumbnail & Storyboard

Rough sketches defining key acting beats, camera angles, comedy timing, and silhouette clarity before animating.

Stage: Planning
02

Rough Keys & Breakdowns

Posing primary storytelling golden poses and arcs with strong squash, stretch, and snappy frame holds.

Stage: Keyframing
03

Tie-Down & Clean Line

Locking precise character model volume, solid linework, and inbetween frame transitions on 2s and 1s.

Stage: Inbetweening
04

Color, Shading & Comp

Flat cel palettes, rim lights, atmospheric environmental shadows, and camera depth of field passes.

Stage: Final Render
01

Reference & Audio Beat

Analyzing live-action video reference, scrubbing audio syllables for lip-sync accents, and planning beats.

Stage: Analysis
02

Stepped Blocking

Keying primary story poses with stepped tangents: silhouette clarity, hips center of gravity, and eye lines.

Stage: Posing
03

Splining & Arc Polish

Switching Euler curves to spline: smoothing motion arcs on wrists and nose tip, and timing follow-through.

Stage: Polish
04

Lighting & Post

NPR toon shaders, stylized rim highlights, motion blur, and final composite passes ready for export.

Stage: Output
How We Collaborate

Work With Me

Whether you need an entire animation sequence from scratch or focused character animation for your game, here is what working together looks like:

1. Scope & Brief

We discuss your vision, visual style (2D hand-drawn, 3D stepped, or Zack D style), target frame rate, and timeline milestones.

Deliverable: Quote & Schedule

2. Work-In-Progress

You get regular review checkpoints: rough storyboard/blocking first, followed by splining or tie-down lines, ensuring zero surprises.

Deliverable: Review Previews

3. Final Delivery

Master animation clips exported in ProRes/MP4, root-motion FBX game assets, layered PSDs, and project archives ready for production.

Deliverable: Master Production Files
