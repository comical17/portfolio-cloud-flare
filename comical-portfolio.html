03 // Behind The Scenes

2D & 3D Animation Process

Switch between my 2D Frame-by-Frame and 3D Character Animation workflows to see how an idea goes from rough napkin doodle to final render.

1

Rough Thumbnails & Beats

Gesture-driven storyboards focusing on silhouette clarity, comedic timing, and Comical’s expressive body language.

2

Keyframes & Breakdowns

Establishing golden poses, squash-and-stretch arcs, and overlapping action on his hair spikes and hanging glasses.

3

Clean Ink & Tie-Down

Consistent line-weight cleanup ensuring off-model distortions are refined while keeping the hand-drawn energy alive.

4

Cel Color & Compositing

Flat fills, directional rim shadows, camera shake, and crisp sound-sync export ready for broadcast or social reels.

1

Orthographic Sculpt & Retopo

Blocking volumes from Comical’s Front/Side/Back sheets and retopologizing clean quads for smooth deformation.

2

IK/FK Rigging & Blendshapes

Custom facial shape keys for Comical’s signature expressions plus dynamic spring bones for his collar glasses.

3

Stepped Blocking to Spline

Animating on 2s in stepped mode for snappy 2D-inspired timing before polishing arcs in the graph editor.

4

NPR Toon Lighting & Render

Inverted-hull outlines, custom shadow ramps, and multi-pass AOV compositing for a vibrant 2.5D finish.
